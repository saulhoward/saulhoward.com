## Talks

### Why Your Enterprise Tech Stack Isn't Ready for AI Agents – And What to Build Instead

[![Video thumbnail for the AI Engineer World's Fair 2026 talk]({static}/images/aie-2026-talk.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mav15aW9lLM)

<p class="entry-meta">AI Engineer World's Fair, San Francisco · AI in Healthcare track · July 2, 2026<br>
Co-presented with Christopher Lovejoy (Anthropic)</p>

Chris and I explained why healthcare AI agents that work as a proof of concept stall on the way to production, and what to build instead: audit trails built on an immutable event log, safe handling of PHI, treating humans and LLMs as the same kind of agent, and evals to keep it all honest.
